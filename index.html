About

I'm a CMO / VP Marketing who builds acquisition engines from zero. I led marketing for eToro's crypto vertical as it became the company's #1 revenue engine, and built XBO's marketing department from the ground up. That's 15+ years in marketing, including 5 in regulated trading and brokerage, managing budgets of $30M+/year with full-funnel ownership — onboarding optimization, CRM automation and performance marketing.

Today I build agentic AI systems that run real marketing work — research, content, reporting and campaign operations — and train teams to work the same way. I manage cross-functional teams, agencies and partnerships in global B2C, B2B and Web3 environments.
